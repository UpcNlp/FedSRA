# Data

- **CIFAR-10 / CIFAR-100 / Tiny-ImageNet**: auto-downloaded by torchvision on first run.
- **MedMNIST (PathMNIST, OrganAMNIST, ...)**: `.npz` files from the `medmnist` package;
  place under `data/medmnist/`.
- **Fed-ISIC2019**: obtained through the FLamby benchmark
  (https://github.com/owkin/FLamby) under its data-usage terms. We do not
  redistribute the clinical images. Place the exported parquet files under
  `data/Fed-ISIC2019/`.
